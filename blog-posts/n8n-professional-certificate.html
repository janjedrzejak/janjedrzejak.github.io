n8n Professional Certificate: building on practice

07 SEP 20262 MINJAN JĘDRZEJAK

In September 2026, I earned the Professional Certificate in n8n Program Completion from n8n Academy. The certificate confirms that I completed all three courses and received passing grades. It is a useful addition to the practical work I already do with automation.

From Power Automate to n8n

After working with Power Automate, I moved to n8n, which has become my main platform for building automation. Today, my projects combine API integrations, databases, automated business reporting, and AI assistants that use business data and context.

That is how the change shows up in my day-to-day work: connecting the source data, the processing logic, and the result someone needs to use. Understanding the business process remains part of the implementation at every step.

Three courses, one connected practice

Completing the program gave me a structured opportunity to review the areas that come together in these projects:

  • Essentials: Your First Workflows. Working with triggers, understanding how data moves between nodes, and mapping and transforming it into the format the next step expects.
  • Integrations: APIs & Connected Workflows. Connecting external services through APIs and webhooks, managing authentication, controlling workflow logic, and handling failed calls.
  • In Practice: AI, Testing & Best Practices. Building AI workflows, choosing between chains and agents, and testing, debugging and organizing workflows for ongoing use.

A result people can rely on

A successful execution is one signal to check. I also want to know whether the result is complete, correct and useful. Three questions help me keep that visible:

  • Data: did the workflow process every expected record, and do the fields and values mean the same thing across systems?
  • Integrations: what happens when an API times out or returns an unexpected response, and how does someone find out?
  • AI: which step benefits from a model, and which can be handled with clearly defined rules and a predictable result?

These questions connect technical implementation with the needs of the people using the outcome. They are also what I want to keep developing as I apply the learning from this program.
